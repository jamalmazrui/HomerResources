## Introduction {#introduction}

Homer Resources gathers everything published under the Homer brand: free
software and reference material by a blind developer, for people who work by
keyboard and screen reader.

There are three kinds — collections of documents you download, directories
you read on the web, and tools you install. Each kind has its own page,
linked from the section heading below.

## [Homer Collections][p-collections] {#homer-collections}

Each collection puts public material that is hard to read with a screen
reader somewhere readable. Source documents come with HTML and plain text
copies, and each heading below downloads the whole collection as a zip
archive.

### [A11yAI][c-a11yai-zip] {#c-a11yai}

About 60 documents on accessibility and artificial intelligence, including
readable guides to ChatGPT, Copilot, Gemini, and other AI engines.

### [Access ICT][c-accessict-zip] {#c-accessict}

More than 100 documents on accessible information and communication
technology, covering assistive technology, design, documents, captions,
regulations, testing, and many other topics.

### [App Help Guides][c-apphelpguides-zip] {#c-apphelpguides}

Publishers' own help documentation for 63 products in 12 categories,
gathered into one document each. A help site buries a short answer under
banners and widgets, while one document has a single heading structure, a
single search, and works offline.

### [DevDocs][c-devdocs-zip] {#c-devdocs}

More than 80 tutorials and references for developers, covering Python,
JavaScript, C#, Git, SQL, and many other languages and tools.

## [Homer Directories][p-directories] {#homer-directories}

Each directory saves the reader the search: someone has already visited the
sources, checked the links, and written down what was found. Each is one web
page under headings, in alphabetical order, with appendixes for browsing a
second way.

### [AudioVault][d-audiovault] {#d-audiovault}

Movies and television shows that have audio description, each title linking
to its audio download, with year, genres, running time, and IMDb rating.

### [Blind Authors][d-authors] {#d-authors}

59 writers who are blind or have low vision, and 86 books they wrote on their
own, each available as electronic text or audio.

### [Blind Creators][d-creators] {#d-creators}

115 people who author books, develop apps, or make presentations, merging
Blind Authors, Blind Developers, and Blind Presenters so that each person
appears once.

### [Blind Developers][d-developers] {#d-developers}

32 software developers who are blind or have low vision, and the software
each has built.

### [Blind Movers][d-movers] {#d-movers}

Travel, recreation, and tourism while blind or low vision: the organizations,
tours, and services that plan for blind travelers.

### [Blind Presenters][d-presenters] {#d-presenters}

36 people who lead in podcasts, talks, video, broadcast, and film, from 2010
onward.

### [Tech Training][d-techtraining] {#d-techtraining}

1304 free video and audio presentations on technology and accessibility, from
25 sources in 20 topic categories, each linking straight to the presentation.

## [Homer Tools][p-tools] {#homer-tools}

The apps do different jobs but are built the same way:

- 64-bit Windows programs, written in C# or Python, MIT-licensed, with source
  code on GitHub.
- Most run as a dialog or from the command line, with the same options either
  way, named consistently across apps, so hand work can be scripted.
- One simple installer each, offered here as a direct download.
- Speech through JAWS and NVDA directly, and a Windows notification for other
  screen readers.
- Work done on your computer, not on a server.

Each ships the same documents, in HTML and Markdown: **ReadMe** for a quick
start, **Announce** for what is new, its **own guide** for full reference,
**Developer** for how it is built, **History** for changes, and **Hotkeys**
for every keystroke.

Each heading below links to the app's project page.

### [2htm][t-2htm] {#t-2htm}

Converts Word, Excel, and PowerPoint files to clean HTML, keeping the
headings, lists, tables, and reading order a screen reader needs.
[Installer][t-2htm-setup].

### [bookFido][t-bookfido] {#t-bookfido}

One catalog of your books across Audible, Bookshare, Goodreads, Kindle, and
NLS BARD, read through your own signed-in Microsoft Edge, with nothing about
your accounts leaving the computer.
[Installer][t-bookfido-setup].

### [DbDo][t-dbdo] {#t-dbdo}

Manages SQLite, Access, Excel, and other databases from the keyboard, as
Windows lists you move through cell by cell or a dot prompt for commands and
SQL.
[Installer][t-dbdo-setup].

### [EdSharp][t-edsharp] {#t-edsharp}

An editor for text and program code, in use since 2007, that speaks the
result of each command, with snippets, structured text, math, and scripting
add-ins.
[Installer][t-edsharp-setup].

### [extCheck][t-extcheck] {#t-extcheck}

Checks Word, Excel, PowerPoint, and Markdown files against rules from the
Microsoft Office Accessibility Checker and the accessibility guidelines for
the web, a whole folder at a time.
[Installer][t-extcheck-setup].

### [FileDir][t-filedir] {#t-filedir}

Manages files and folders by typed command rather than by mouse, in use since
2006.
[Installer][t-filedir-setup].

### [HomerScribe][t-homerscribe] {#t-homerscribe}

Describes video and transcribes audio, returning a copy of the film with
description mixed into its sound. The AI models run on your own machine, so
there is no upload and no fee.
[Installer][t-homerscribe-setup].

### [HomerView][t-homerview] {#t-homerview}

An NVDA add-on that asks Microsoft Edge directly what is on a page: counts of
headings and links, accessibility problems, where a link really goes, and the
main article without the clutter.
[Installer][t-homerview-setup].

### [urlCheck][t-urlcheck] {#t-urlcheck}

Tests web pages with the axe-core engine and drafts an Accessibility
Conformance Report over all 86 WCAG 2.2 criteria, working through a whole
list unattended.
[Installer][t-urlcheck-setup].

### [urlFido][t-urlfido] {#t-urlfido}

Downloads every file of a chosen kind from a web page, driving your own
Microsoft Edge so that sign-ins apply.
[Installer][t-urlfido-setup].
